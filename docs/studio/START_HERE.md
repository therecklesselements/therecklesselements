## How work moves

The creator's latest explicit decision governs. Apply approved studio doctrine within its scope and the game's own controls within theirs. A conflict affecting canon, scope, rights or acceptance goes to the creator. Keep proposals and historical examples labelled until a decision settles them.

FATDOG is the sole active game-production project through Alpha completion, final implementation and polish, and creator-accepted Beta readiness. The creator authorizes each transition. Preparation for later properties is limited to source recovery, briefs and rights work, or bounded read-only research.

Begin with the player experience. Reuse the closest suitable, commercially permitted working foundation. Give each system one owner and use existing authoring tools first. Demonstrate the defining interaction in a playable build. Where content editing is part of the milestone, show that a designer can produce a variation through the existing controls. Refine identity, feel and polish under creator direction.

The project lead owns one checkout and its Editor writes. Each handoff identifies the authorized outcome, controlling source and revision, affected path, smallest meaningful check, evidence, open decisions and next owner. Keep the original conditions with every reused result. At a release gate, identify the exact packaged build tested and record the creator's acceptance separately.

Keep proprietary source, working portfolio material, rights evidence and machine-specific records in their authorized private locations.
